Revenue Leak Prevention

Where Practices Lose Money

Revenue leaks happen at many points across the care cycle. StelleMed checks for every one of these, so no revenue is left behind.

1

Unbilled Visits

Patient encounters that take place but are never coded, billed, or submitted, leading to permanent revenue loss.

2

Denials Not Appealed

Legitimate clinical claims rejected by payers that staff simply write off instead of preparing clinical appeals.

3

Underpayments

Insurers reimbursing below contracted fee schedule rates without being audited or challenged by the practice.

4

Patient Balances Unpaid

High deductibles and co-pays left uncollected due to lack of upfront estimates and convenient digital payment options.

5

Missed Filing Deadlines

Claims delayed past payer timely filing limits, resulting in unappealable, total forfeiture of reimbursement.

6

Payments Not Posted

Checks and ERAs sitting unposted, distorting financial balances and preventing accurate secondary billing.

7

Claims Rejected

Clearinghouse front-end format and demographic rejections that stall before reaching the payer adjudicator.

8

No Response on Claims

Claims lost in payer black holes where no follow-up is initiated, allowing carrier statutory hold periods to lapse.
