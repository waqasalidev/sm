Front-End & Patient Financial Care

The 10 Patient & Practice Offerings

Protect patient relationships while increasing self-pay recovery. We provide empathetic communication that turns confusing medical bills into clear payment solutions.

1

Patient Medical Record

Accurate demographic and medical record capture, ensuring clean clinical histories, compliant chart access, and seamless insurance cross-referencing.

  • Precise patient intake data auditing to prevent front-end billing discrepancies
  • Secure electronic health record indexing and HIPAA-compliant archiving
  • Streamlined chart retrieval for payer medical necessity reviews
2

Payment Plans & Self-Pay Collections

Flexible, customized monthly installment plans and recurring card-on-file schedules that empower patients to pay balances without creating bad debt.

  • Budget-friendly recurring auto-debit arrangements
  • PCI-DSS compliant encrypted card-on-file tokenization
  • Compassionate self-pay recovery protocols that preserve doctor-patient trust
3

Cost Estimates

Transparent Good Faith Estimates (GFE) provided prior to scheduled clinical services, giving patients upfront clarity and maintaining full compliance with the No Surprises Act.

  • Pre-service deductible, copay, and coinsurance calculation
  • Itemized cost estimates for planned diagnostics and procedures
  • Reduction of post-treatment billing confusion and disputed charges
4

Patient Billing Support

Dedicated, empathetic billing customer service answering patient questions regarding invoices, insurance balances, and Explanation of Benefits (EOB).

  • Toll-free patient support line answered by trained medical billing representatives
  • Bilingual communication support for diverse patient communities
  • Rapid first-contact resolution for billing and balance inquiries
5

Legacy A/R Recovery

Targeted recovery projects for aged self-pay and delinquent practice receivables that have lingered over 60, 90, or 120+ days.

  • Audit and cleanup of stale or unresolved patient balances
  • Ethical outreach campaigns via SMS, email, and clear paper statements
  • Substantial cash recovery from written-off or forgotten receivables
6

Front-Office & Scheduling Assistance

Intake workflows and front-desk coaching to collect correct insurance cards, copays, and demographic data at the point of arrival.

  • Co-pay and deductible collection scripts for reception staff
  • Optimization of appointment scheduling templates to minimize patient no-shows
  • Integration of digital intake check-in kiosks and tablet forms
7

Patient Invoicing & Statements

Clear, modern, jargon-free monthly statements distributed electronically via SMS text message, encrypted email, and printed mail.

  • Easy-to-read invoices showing insurance payments and remaining patient portion
  • Multi-channel statement delivery to accelerate patient response times
  • Prompt automated statement cycles sent within 48 hours of payer adjudication
8

Online Payment Portals

Branded 24/7 web and mobile payment gateways allowing patients to conveniently pay balances via credit card, HSA/FSA, Apple Pay, or Google Pay.

  • Seamless 1-click payment links directly inside text statement reminders
  • Zero-registration guest checkout options to eliminate payment friction
  • Automated instant payment posting into practice management software
9

Physician Billing & Specialty Routing

Specialized clinical billing workflows tailored for single-provider practices, multi-specialty physician clinics, and hospital-employed medical groups.

  • Accurate tracking of physician productivity and relative value units (RVUs)
  • Specialty-specific billing rules for surgery, cardiology, pediatrics, and medicine
  • Customized monthly physician compensation and collections reporting
10

Practice Management & Consulting

Strategic operational advisory analyzing clinical scheduling efficiency, staff overhead, fee schedule competitiveness, and clinical documentation improvement.

  • Benchmarking practice collections against national MGMA specialty standards
  • Comprehensive payer fee schedule negotiation and contract evaluation
  • Identification of operational bottlenecks to maximize clinical profitability
