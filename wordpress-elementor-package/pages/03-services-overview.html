Medical Specialty Expertise

Tailored for Your Clinical Specialty

Every medical discipline operates under unique payer guidelines, documentation hurdles, and fee schedules. Our certified billing teams have dedicated specialty pods.

Internal & Family Medicine

Complex multi-code visits, preventive wellness (AWV), chronic care management (CCM).

Cardiology & Vascular

Cath lab interventions, echocardiography, nuclear imaging, and modifier 25/59 optimization.

Orthopedics & Sports Medicine

Surgical bundles, implant billing, DME pre-authorizations, and physical therapy cross-overs.

Mental & Behavioral Health

Psychotherapy, IOP, medication management, and specialized telehealth coding rules.

Pediatrics & Adolescent Care

Vaccine administration bundles, developmental screenings, and Medicaid managed care plans.

General & Ambulatory Surgery

ASC billing, anesthesia coordination, pre-op clearance, and high-dollar claim appeals.

Urgent Care Centers

High-volume rapid charge capture, same-day verification, and occupational medicine billing.

Physical & Occupational Therapy

Timed 8-minute rule calculations, functional progress reporting, and plan-of-care caps.
